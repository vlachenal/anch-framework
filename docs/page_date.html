AnCH date library documentation

Introduction

AnCH date library aims to provide facilities for applications manipulate date/time.

Prerequisites

None

Installation

CMake target anch::date:

find_package(Anch REQUIRED)
target_link_libraries(my-app PRIVATE anch::date)

See installation.

Usage

anch::date::Date stores a point in time (nanoseconds since epoch) and its local time fields. It can be built from now (default constructor), std::chrono::system_clock::time_point , std::time_t , std::tm (local time) and std::timespec , and explicitly converted to these types. Dates are compared with their timestamp.
Local time follows the TZ environment variable, daylight saving time included.

anch::date::DateFormatter formats and parses dates with patterns: %Y , %y , %m , %d , %H , %h (12 hours), %p (a.m./p.m.), %M , %S and %s (milliseconds). Other patterns can be added by implementing anch::date::IDatePartFormatter and registering it with anch::date::DateFormatter::registerFormatterPart .
Parsing is strict: every field has a fixed size, constant parts have to match and impossible dates are refused.

For simple formatting, std::format with std::chrono specifiers can also be used on the converted time point.

anch::date::rfc3339 formats and parses RFC 3339 dates (JSON schema and OpenAPI date-time and date formats): date-time is a anch::date::Date (written in UTC or with the local offset), full-date is a std::chrono::year_month_day . anch-json uses them for these types when ANCH_DATE is ON .

Example

#include "date/dateFormatter.hpp"
anch::date::DateFormatter formatter("%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S.%s");
std::string str;
formatter.format(anch::date::Date(), str); // now
anch::date::Date date = formatter.parse("2012-10-23 10:02:59.998");
std::time_t time = static_cast<std::time_t>(date);
