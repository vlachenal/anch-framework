Detailed Description

Date and time utility class.

A Date is a point in time with a nanosecond precision (stored as a number of nanoseconds since epoch, which covers years 1678 to 2261), and its broken down representation in local time (year, month, day, hour, minute, second, millisecond, microsecond and nanosecond).
Local time is computed with localtime_r (std::chrono time zones are not available with GCC 13): it is thread safe and follows the TZ environment variable.
Date can be converted from and to std::chrono::system_clock::time_point , std::time_t , std::tm and std::timespec .
